About

A tiny bot with
a very specific job.

Markito started from a simple thought: if you're already online all day, a machine should be able to handle the repetitive checking and leave the judgment to you.

“Watch more sources than a person reasonably can. Then tell the person less.”

That is the whole product thesis.
Product principles

What Markito should feel like.

Quiet by default

Alerts should earn the interruption. Everything else can wait for a digest.

Source-aware

An official disclosure, professional report and social post should never be presented as if they carry identical evidentiary weight.

Human in the loop

Markito can detect, organize and summarize. It should not pretend to eliminate the need for judgment.

Measurable

Signals should be logged so usefulness can be tested later instead of judged by a couple of memorable wins.

No fake sophistication

No pointless dashboards, fabricated “AI confidence” meters or dozens of indicators just to make the product look expensive.

Mascot

Yep, he's literally a black square.

Two eyes. One line for a mouth. That is enough. The visual identity stays deliberately simple so Markito feels like a useful little tool instead of another over-designed finance platform.

Build order

Scanner first. Fancy stuff later.
