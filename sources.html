Markito makes information easier to notice without hiding where it came from or pretending every source deserves equal weight.

Regulatory filings

Official disclosure systems, new forms and meaningful changes between versions.

Investor relations

Company announcements, earnings materials, presentations and first-party updates.

Market data

Price, volume and public activity data used to identify unusual movement.

Professional news

Selected reporting sources, grouped so one event does not become ten alerts.

Project & exchange updates

Public status pages, release notes and official notices where they add useful context.

Public social context

Selected public accounts or communities, clearly separated from primary disclosures.

Not every source gets the same weight.

An official filing, a news report and a social post should never collapse into one mysterious “AI confidence” score.

Primary

Regulators, issuers, exchanges and first-party announcements.

Secondary

Professional reporting and structured market-data providers.

Contextual

Public communities and social sources, labeled for what they are.

Summarize the source. Never replace it.
