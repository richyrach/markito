How it works

Three steps. Nothing weird.

Markito is designed like a scanner, not a trading terminal. It watches, filters and explains; you decide what to do next.

1

Choose what matters

Pick tickers, sectors, crypto assets or themes you want Markito to keep an eye on.

2

Markito scans

Public sources are checked for filings, announcements, headlines, unusual activity and relevant changes.

3

You get the signal

Important items become short, readable alerts with the original source kept one click away.
