Sources

Public first.
Source attached.

Markito should make it easier to notice information without hiding where it came from. Every important alert should preserve a timestamp and a path back to the original public source.

§

Regulatory filings

Company filings and official disclosure systems, including new forms and meaningful changes between versions.

◫

Investor relations

Company announcements, earnings materials, presentations and other first-party public updates.

⌁

Market data

Price, volume and other public data used to detect abnormal movement relative to recent conditions.

N

News

Selected public news sources, normalized so repeated reporting of the same event does not become alert spam.

◎

Project & exchange updates

For crypto and technology: public status pages, release notes, repository releases and official exchange notices where appropriate.

@

Public social sources

Selected public accounts or communities can provide context, but should be labeled separately from official disclosures.

Source hierarchy

Not every source deserves the same weight.

An official filing is different from a repost, rumor or anonymous comment. Markito should show that distinction instead of blending everything into one confidence-looking score.

Primary

Regulators, issuers, exchanges and first-party announcements.

Secondary

Professional reporting and structured market-data providers.

Contextual

Public communities and social sources, clearly labeled and verified where possible.

Principle

Summarize the source. Never replace it.
