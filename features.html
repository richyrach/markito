Features

See what changed.
Skip what didn't.

Markito is intentionally narrow: monitor public information, highlight useful changes and keep the source close enough to verify.

↗
Core

Signal detection

Rules can flag unusual changes in price, volume, filings, headlines or source activity. The goal is not to predict the market; it is to surface things worth investigating.

≡
Core

News & filing monitor

Watch official company pages, regulatory filings and selected news sources without manually checking every feed yourself.

☆
Personal

Watchlists

Follow specific companies, crypto assets, sectors or topics. Markito should become quieter as your interests become more specific.

◎
Context

Signal history

Log what was detected and what happened afterward, so you can review whether a type of alert was useful instead of relying on memory.

⌁
Clarity

Short summaries

Compress long updates into a quick explanation, with the original link and timestamp still visible for verification.

⌁
Later

Paper-trade experiments

Test ideas against simulated positions before any real-money workflow is even considered. No automatic execution in the early version.

Deliberately not a terminal

Less interface. More context.

There is no fake dashboard stuffed with twenty charts just to look “financial.” The site explains the product; the product itself can stay focused on alerts, sources and review.

Clear timestamps

Know exactly when a source changed and when Markito noticed it.

Original-source links

Do not trust a summary blindly. Open the source.

Configurable noise

Decide what deserves an alert and what belongs in a digest.
